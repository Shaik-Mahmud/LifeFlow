Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create or modify your account, request services, contact customer support, or otherwise communicate with us. This includes name, email, phone number, and blood group.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect primarily to facilitate the matching of blood donors with those in need. We may also use it to communicate with you about emergency requests in your area.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and destruction.
