Why it matters

A small moment of care can mean everything.

People need blood every day—during surgeries, childbirth, cancer treatment, accidents and many other urgent situations.

There is no substitute

Hospitals rely on voluntary donors because blood cannot be made in a lab or stored forever.

Need is always changing

Regular donors help keep a diverse blood supply ready for patients with every blood type.

Your impact is real

A single donation can support multiple people through blood components used in different treatments.

Simple from start to finish

What happens when you donate?

Donating blood is usually a straightforward process, guided by trained healthcare professionals.

Start your donor profile
01

Register and check in

Create your donor profile and share the information needed to assess eligibility.

02

Complete a quick health check

A trained team reviews your wellbeing and makes sure donation is safe for you.

03

Donate and recover

The donation itself is brief. Afterwards, take time to rest and enjoy refreshments.

Before you donate

Check that you’re ready to give.

Eligibility rules can differ by location and medical history. In general, donors should be healthy, well-rested and meet local age and weight requirements.

Feel healthy on donation day Bring a valid ID Eat and drink beforehand

Always follow guidance from your local blood centre or healthcare provider before donating.
