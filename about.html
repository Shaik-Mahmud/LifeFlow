About LifeFlow

Connecting generosity with urgent need.

We make it easier for donors, patients and hospitals to come together when every minute matters.

Our purpose

A faster path from a call for help to a willing donor.

LifeFlow is a blood-donation coordination platform built around one simple belief: finding compatible blood should not be harder than it has to be.

By bringing requests, donor availability and essential details into one clear place, we help communities respond with more confidence, compassion and speed.
